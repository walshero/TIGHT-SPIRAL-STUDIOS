The craft/reception seat's verdict: **manufactured-silent-majority earns the turn; Cambodia is inert to the encoded object.** The player rates the *silent-majority appeal itself*; the manufactured-majority reveal reconsolidates exactly that trace (the "reasonable center" you were flattered to join was the engineered product). JFK and LBJ both pass the "reveal operates on the rated quality" test; Cambodia is the one scene that fails it.

Two caveats the decision must carry:
- **Mission vs mechanic.** Cambodia is the stronger *mission* fit ("what was said vs what was true"); silent-majority is the stronger *mechanic* fit (the spine: two readings of the same message). The spine is the mechanic — mechanic should win — but state it, don't bury it.
- **Honesty cost.** Cambodia is unimpeachable declassified fact. The silent-majority reveal is more interpretive and, if written as editorial ("it was all fake"), becomes itself a persuasion move. It ships only if grounded in dated, attributed record (speechwriting/strategy memos to marginalize dissent; the orchestrated RNC telegram/letter campaign; contemporaneous polling that shaped the "silent majority" label).
