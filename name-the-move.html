Name the Move — a critical thinking lab for CT100, MassBay Community College.

Archival clips are verbatim excerpts from public-domain speeches and texts; each carries its speaker, year, licence and source link. Composed clips are original scripts written for this lab, with invented speakers, places and figures. No fabricated quotation is attributed to any living person.

The Record Room ships no quotations at all. It lists standing public-domain sources — works of the United States Government are not subject to copyright under 17 U.S.C. §105, and judicial opinions are uncopyrightable — together with the portal, the session, the citation form and directions to the text. You fetch the passage; nothing is stored here and nothing goes stale when a session ends.

The scorecards follow the structure of the AAC&U VALUE rubrics for Ethical Reasoning and Critical Thinking (Association of American Colleges & Universities). The performance descriptors here are original to this lab and adapted for public speech rather than student work; for programme-level assessment of student artefacts, use the official VALUE rubrics.

Everything you do here stays in this browser. There is no account, no server and no upload; clearing site data erases your work, so export before you switch machines.
