We build instruments that show you the rules while they are being run on you.

Last updated: —

Most software hides its machinery. Ours hands it to you and lets you turn it. You pull the levers, then the seat turns around and shows you your own telemetry.

Some of these are games. Some are assessment tools designed for a college. One is designed for a Manhattan relocation firm. They are the same engine wearing different clothes: make the invisible constraint visible, and let the person find it rather than be told.
