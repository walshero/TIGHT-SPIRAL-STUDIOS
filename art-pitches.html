Tight Spiral Productions — Art Review

Three directions for the studio marks

The same three weak marks — the studio machine, the system map, and the MassBay wheel — redrawn in each direction. Judge the craft, then pick a lane; I redraw the full set to the winner.

All SVG, single-file, offline, no emoji, ink on paper (AA-safe). Line weights and solids chosen for low-vision legibility, not just for looks.

A · Technical Engraving

Instrument-grade

Patent-drawing precision. Dual stroke weights — a heavy contour carries the shape, fine interior lines add mechanism. Reads as something you'd trust to measure. Closest to the existing dial hero, so it unifies with what's shipped.

RP: heavy 3px contour holds at small sizes; fine detail is bonus, never load-bearing.

B · Tight Spiral

Namesake motif

Every mark is built from the studio's own spiral. One organizing gesture across the whole set — brand cohesion and a mnemonic: the tightening coil is the studio's idea made visible. Most distinctive; least like a stock icon set.

RP: single continuous 3.2px stroke, high figure/ground separation, no fine interior clutter.

C · Cut-Paper Relief

Solid · warm

Solid shapes with a single offset shadow layer — bold, warm, tactile. Descends from the lumière cut-paper heritage already in the studio. Heaviest visual weight of the three, and the most legible at a glance.

RP: solid fills beat thin strokes for low vision — the largest, highest-contrast target of the three directions.
