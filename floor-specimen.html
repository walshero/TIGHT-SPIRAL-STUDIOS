The verdict

A and B are two steps apart on a ladder with about forty rungs. The gap you feel between them is real but small, and it is the least important variable on this screen.

C is a different kind of thing. It is not a bigger number, it is the reader in control. Nothing in WCAG mandates a minimum pixel size anywhere. WCAG 1.4.4 asks that text scale to 200% without losing content or function. Apple's Larger Text criterion asks the same: enlarge to 200% or the system maximum without overlap or severe truncation.

So a fixed 18px floor is not the accessible choice. It is a guess at one reader, applied to every reader, and it cannot be met or failed by anyone but the designer. The test with teeth is: does the screen survive 200%?

Move this. Nothing was imposed on you — that is the whole argument.
