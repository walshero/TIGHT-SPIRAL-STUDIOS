# ART DOCTRINE — Tight Spiral Studios
*Founder ruling, 2026-07-30. Governs every image in the studio.*

## SCAFFOLDING, NOT MASTERPIECES

The current art pipeline is the manual process for sketchy graphics, named plainly:

1. The founder generates references in Midjourney (his subscription, his picks, his eye).
2. The machine traces them to SVG (vtracer, posterized paths) or hand-authors vector
   approximations. Gated for contrast in all three light modes, offline, no emoji.
3. Provenance is written into each scene's aria-label.

**None of this is precious.** It is scaffolding that holds the wall up until a person
with hands arrives. The Borges piece (hero + five rooms) is the reference implementation.

## MAXIMUM HITL — THE OPEN CHAIR

The studio goal is maximum human-in-the-loop. There is a standing, open invitation to
artists: **any machine-originated mark in this studio is an open chair.** Take over any
Midjourney-traced scene or Claude-drawn SVG, redraw it as yours, and your authorship
replaces the machine's — credited in the file, permanently. The floors still apply
(contrast gated in Default/Dusk/Warm-dark, single-file, offline, no emoji); the art
inside the floors is the artist's.

Machine art never blocks a human artist and is never defended against one.

## BRAND SYSTEM — founder ruling

- **The logo is the tight spiral.** Identity mark, first position, unchanged.
- **The teaspoon is the imprint mark of SUPER SKETCHY GRAPHICS** — the studio's
  machine-art subsidiary, a gear of TSP. Every machine-originated artwork (Midjourney
  trace, Claude SVG) carries the teaspoon imprint until an artist takes the chair.
  The teaspoon marks provenance, never studio identity — identity is the spiral, always.
- **The measure ladder is the growth motif: tsp → tbsp → cup.** Small truths compound
  into bigger vessels. Use it wherever the studio explains how work scales — a kernel
  becomes a game becomes a suite.

Hierarchy in practice: where one mark identifies the studio, use the spiral. Where the
studio explains its method, the teaspoon and the ladder carry it.

## PROVENANCE RULES (unchanged, restated)

- Every artwork carries its chain: who generated, who traced, who redrew.
- MJ generations are the founder's (his subscription); traces and integration are studio
  work; human redraws are the artist's, credited.
- Student work and client materials never enter the art corpus, same as the text bank.
