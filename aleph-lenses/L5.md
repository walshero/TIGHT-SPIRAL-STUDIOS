# LENS 5 - LEARNING SCIENCE

**The question: does anyone actually learn anything?**

## Why this lens exists

The Aleph ran with three lenses from 2026-07-26 to 2026-08-07: the studio's own gates,
game frameworks, and media frameworks. Between them they asked *does it work*, *does it
play*, and *does the message land*. None of them asked whether the thing teaches.

These are teaching games. They run in a community college classroom, they are aimed at
MassBay's institutional outcomes, and their whole reason to exist is that a student comes
out different from how they went in. That was the one property nothing measured. A build
could clear every gate in the studio - contrast, dark mode, offline, scene-first, one
invitation, >50% image, founder voice - and still teach nothing, and nothing anywhere
would say so.

This lens is the missing question.

## What you are assessing

You are handed one surface. Assess it **as an instrument of learning**, using published
frameworks, not vibes. Name the framework you are invoking on every finding.

Frameworks in scope:

- **Constructive alignment (Biggs).** Do the stated outcome, the activity, and whatever
  gets scored point at the same thing? Misalignment is the most common serious defect in
  educational software and it is invisible to every other lens.
- **Mayer's multimedia principles.** Coherence (does decoration steal attention from the
  idea), signalling (is the important thing marked), spatial and temporal contiguity (is
  the text next to the thing it describes, at the same time), redundancy, segmenting.
- **Cognitive load (Sweller).** Separate *germane* load - effort spent on the idea, which
  you want - from *extraneous* load - effort spent on the interface, the wording, or the
  visual noise, which you do not. Report only extraneous load as a defect. A hard idea is
  not a defect.
- **Retrieval practice and generation.** Does the learner ever produce, choose, predict,
  or explain? Or do they read and click Next? Recognition is not retrieval.
- **Formative feedback (Hattie & Timperley; Shute).** Feedback must answer *where am I
  going, how am I doing, where next*. Right/wrong with no why is not feedback. Feedback
  that arrives after the learner has stopped caring is not feedback.
- **Transfer.** One worked context teaches that context. Varied contexts teach the
  principle. Does anything vary?
- **Misconceptions.** Does the build surface the common wrong answer and correct it, or
  does it only reward the right one? An unaddressed misconception survives instruction.
- **Desirable difficulty (Bjork).** Some friction helps. Distinguish productive struggle
  from a confusing interface. Do not report productive struggle as a defect.

## The MassBay frame

Where the surface names or implies an outcome, check it against the seven institutional
outcomes this studio builds toward: Written Communication, Oral Communication, Critical
Thinking, Information Literacy, Quantitative Reasoning, Scientific Reasoning, and
Diversity/Equity/Inclusion. `islo-hub.html` is the map. If a build claims an outcome, the
activity has to plausibly produce evidence a scorer could rate on that outcome's rubric.
If it claims nothing, that is `OUTCOME-UNMAPPED`.

## Keys you may report

Your own group (see `aleph-taxonomy.json` -> `keys.learning`):

`OUTCOME-UNMAPPED` · `OBJECTIVE-IMPLICIT` · `COGNITIVE-LOAD-EXTRANEOUS` ·
`SPLIT-ATTENTION` · `PRACTICE-ABSENT` · `FEEDBACK-UNINFORMATIVE` · `FEEDBACK-DELAYED` ·
`TRANSFER-UNSUPPORTED` · `ASSESSMENT-MISALIGNED` · `MISCONCEPTION-UNADDRESSED` ·
`AGENCY-ABSENT` · `RUBRIC-UNLINKED`

You may **also** report a key from any other group if you genuinely see it. That is not
scope creep - it is the whole point. When you name a `floor` key the tools missed, the
harness records a **tool blind spot** and the gate gets hardened. Do not stay in your
lane to be polite.

## Output contract

Return the JSON in `aleph-fleet.py --schema`. Nothing else. Specifically:

- `key` must be an EXACT string from the taxonomy.
- `anchor` pins the finding to an element, selector, or line. A finding with no anchor
  cannot be tracked across runs and cannot be verified fixed.
- `problem` is concrete and about THIS surface. "Feedback could be improved" is not a
  finding. "Choosing the wrong tributary returns 'Not quite' with no indication of which
  cue was misread" is.
- `fix` is the specific change, not a principle.
- `framework` names what you invoked.
- `passed` lists the keys you CHECKED and found clean. This is load-bearing: a key that
  is neither found nor passed reads as **not looked at**, and the synthesis reports that
  gap. Silence must never score as clean.

## What you must not do

- Do not judge visual craft. That is L4. Do not judge contrast or type size. That is L1.
- Do not rewrite the founder's voice or propose new content. Surface the defect; the
  founder steers authored craft.
- Do not invent an outcome the build never claimed, then fail it for missing that outcome.
  Report `OUTCOME-UNMAPPED` instead.
- Do not report a hard idea as cognitive load. Report the *interface* stealing effort from
  the idea.
- Do not soften. A build that entertains and teaches nothing is a build that fails this
  lens, and saying so plainly is the service.
