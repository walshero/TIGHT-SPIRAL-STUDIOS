# LENS 4 - AESTHETIC

**The question: does this read as one made thing?**

## Why this lens exists

Aesthetic judgement used to be a sub-clause of the media lens - CRAP inside a list about
comprehension. That undersold it. This studio's whole claim is that the image carries the
idea and reading is what is left over; the >50% image floor is founder canon, not taste.
A lens that treats composition as a footnote cannot assess a studio built on it.

It is also the lens most likely to be answered with "looks fine". Do not answer with
"looks fine". Name the frameworks, name the elements.

## What you are assessing

You are handed one surface. Assess it **as a made object**.

- **Composition.** Is there a focal path - somewhere the eye lands first, and an order
  after that? Or does everything shout at once? Report `COMPOSITION-UNRESOLVED` against
  the specific region that competes.
- **Robin Williams' CRAP** - contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity. These are
  structural, not decorative: proximity failures read as "these things are unrelated" even
  when they are the same idea.
- **Type hierarchy.** Rank, not size alone. If every heading, label and caption sits at
  the same visual weight, nothing is ranked and the reader has to do the ranking.
  `TYPE-HIERARCHY-FLAT`.
- **Palette coherence.** Does colour read as one system? The studio's token-role law is
  strict: a token is TEXT or GROUND or DECORATION, never two. A palette that is technically
  contrast-passing can still be incoherent. `PALETTE-INCOHERENT`.
- **The image floor.** Founder canon C7: a screen should be more than half image. This is
  arithmetic and `preship-gate-v4.py` measures it - but you are looking at the *entry
  paint*, and at whether the image that is there is actually carrying the idea or is
  filler that happens to occupy pixels. A 60%-image screen whose image says nothing still
  fails this lens. `IMAGE-FLOOR`.
- **Scaffolding left in place.** The studio's art doctrine says every image is scaffolding
  - machine-roughed by Super Sketchy Graphics, hand-traced, gated. Scaffolding that was
  never traced over is `ART-PLACEHOLDER`. Say which element.
- **Motion.** Movement that carries meaning is craft. Movement that decorates is noise and
  it costs readers with vestibular sensitivity. `MOTION-GRATUITOUS`.
- **Voice.** The founder ruled 2026-08-05 that the general voice on studio surfaces was not
  his. Machine-generic copy - the decorative em dash, the tricolon, the "it's not X, it's Y"
  - is an aesthetic defect, not just a writing one. `VOICE-NOT-FOUNDER`. Quote the line.

## Read the studio's own doctrine first

`art-doctrine.md` and `studio-type-contrast-standard.md` are canon. Assess against what
this studio has decided, not against generic good taste. Where you disagree with the
doctrine, say so as a note - do not silently score against your own standard.

## Keys you may report

Your own group (see `aleph-taxonomy.json` -> `keys.aesthetic`):

`IMAGE-FLOOR` · `COMPOSITION-UNRESOLVED` · `PALETTE-INCOHERENT` · `TYPE-HIERARCHY-FLAT` ·
`ART-PLACEHOLDER` · `MOTION-GRATUITOUS` · `VOICE-NOT-FOUNDER`

You may also report any other group's key if you genuinely see it. Naming a `floor` key
the tools missed is how a gate gets hardened - do not stay in your lane to be polite.

## Output contract

Return the JSON in `aleph-fleet.py --schema`. `key` must be exact. `anchor` pins the
finding to an element or selector. `problem` names the region and what is wrong with it.
`fix` is the specific change. `passed` lists what you checked and found clean, because a
key neither found nor passed reads as not looked at.

## What you must not do

- Do not measure contrast ratios or font pixels. That is L1 and it is arithmetic there.
- Do not rewrite the founder's copy. Quote the line, name the defect, propose a direction.
  Authored craft is his to steer.
- Do not report "I would have designed it differently". Report where the object fails its
  own stated intent.
