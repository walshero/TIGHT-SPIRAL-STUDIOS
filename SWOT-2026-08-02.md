# TSP Studio Strategy — SWOT at the two-month mark (2026-08-02)

*Phase: proof of concept. Bet: ~9:1 engine-over-assets. Moat: the quality layer.
Git captures the recent ~2-week consolidation; the studio work spans ~two months.*

**Standing course-correction folded in (founder ruling, 2026-08-02):** the founder's
vision *informs the defaults, it does not become a universal wall.* WCAG AA is the floor;
the founder's profile is the **default preset** among several selectable profiles; the
individual user's needs win. Codified in OS §3.1 (adaptive-system amendment) and the new
Experience Optimizer seat (§5).

---

## Strengths
- **A machine-enforced quality layer for *craft*, not just code.** Studio Eyes
  (contrast/token-role/focus/touch render gate) + Studio Fingers (touch), a 20-canary
  self-test that refuses to run if it can't grade itself, and a ratchet that took the fleet
  from ~1,149 contrast defects (50 of 52 pages failing) to 47/57 passing, and touch from 43
  failing pages to a handful of documented exceptions. Very few solo studios have CI-grade
  *design* enforcement. This is the defensible asset.
- **Single-file offline HTML as a paying constraint** — portable, durable, no build chain,
  ships to Pages, ages well; tool-chasing guarded structurally (§2).
- **Theory-grounded pedagogy that's genuinely hard to copy** — Gee/Osterweil/Nunan/flow as a
  real spine; "why this teaches" is never hand-wavy.
- **Governance depth + an honesty brand** — a Review Panel of HALT-holding seats catches whole
  failure classes pre-ship; transparent AI-art labeling and no-inflated-claims is a real
  differentiator in an AI-slop market.
- **Accessibility as authentic driver, not checkbox** — credible because it's lived.

## Weaknesses
- **The whole system optimizes around one profile.** §2 names the founder "the living
  accessibility test," and floors encode his eyes. Authentic — but as a *universal* rule it can
  override best practice and a specific user's real needs (the thing this milestone corrects).
  Green-free fleet-wide isn't even gate-enforced (no green check in `studio-eyes.py`) — inconsistent
  policy on top of one-profile policy.
- **Governance is veto, not optimization.** The seats *stop* floor violations. Until today nothing
  *drove* holistic UX quality or measured the positive experience. The OS could tell you a build
  isn't broken; it couldn't tell you it's good to use. (Addressed by the Experience Optimizer seat.)
- **No real per-user adaptivity.** One reduce-motion/bigger-text knob ≠ selectable profiles
  (high-contrast incl. true B/W, colorblind-safe, low-vision, density, motion). `prefers-contrast`
  / `prefers-color-scheme` / `forced-colors` handled ad hoc on a few pages, never systematically
  or gated.
- **No external proof point.** Right focus for PoC, but "the studio is the engine" has zero outside
  evidence yet — no shipped season, no adopter, no outcome.
- **Single point of judgment = bottleneck + bus factor.** Everything routes through one person's
  eyes-voice-judgment by design; scales attention down well, throughput up poorly.
- **Sprawl is live** — 60 live pages (131 HTML total) for a two-month solo PoC is a lot of surface
  to keep green and coherent.

## Opportunities
- **Build the missing layer (now started).** Give interface & holistic experience the same
  theorized rigor content already has (terministic screens / epistemic frames / domain expertise):
  selectable accessibility *profiles*, WCAG as the floor, founder-profile as the default preset.
  Turns the constraint into a *feature*: "adapts to your eyes, not one designer's."
- **Productize the gates.** Studio Eyes/Fingers + the ratchet are the most reusable capital the
  studio has — a "quality OS" other educators/builders would want. Candidate external proof point.
- **Ship a season, not islands (§1.1).** The Noticing collection (CYL, Glass Engine, Flok-adjacent)
  is a latent, legible PoC win.
- **The MJ → Super Sketchy → HITL art pipeline** opens the hero/photoreal lane SVG can't reach, with
  an honest placeholder→human path — new range without dropping the transparency stance.
- **Institutional timing** — MassBay / AI-Task-Force adjacency + accessibility-first + honest-AI is a
  presentable, fundable story *if one build lands as evidence*.

## Threats
- **Accessibility monoculture as liability.** If founder-profile rules harden into universal walls,
  the studio ships experiences optimal for one user and worse (or WCAG-noncompliant) for others — the
  opposite of accessible-first, and legally exposed. This is the risk the 2026-08-02 amendment pre-empts.
- **The moat is also a treadmill.** Every new page is standing maintenance across gates + themes +
  stamps. 60→200 pages without a coherence strategy erodes the very quality that's the moat.
- **Attention burnout** — the one input nothing replaces; the §2 failure modes (tool-chasing,
  optimize-before-ship, too many fronts) are all live at month two.
- **AI-art trust** — even labeled, AI imagery in an accessibility/education brand reads as slop to
  skeptics unless the HITL-replacement promise actually closes.
- **"Beautiful engine, no proof"** — strong internal craft with no adopter/outcome by end of PoC.

---

## The move — accessibility-as-adaptive-system
Founder insight sets the *default preset*; WCAG is the *floor*; the user picks their *profile*.
In priority order:

1. **Codify the amendment** *(done 2026-08-02)* — OS §3.1 rewritten (WCAG floor + selectable
   profiles; founder = default preset, never a universal override); green-free retired as a
   *universal* rule, kept as the default preset + a color-safe profile; "state never by color alone"
   made the universal (WCAG 1.4.1).
2. **Seat the gap** *(done 2026-08-02)* — the **Experience Optimizer** seat (§5): optimizes and
   measures holistic UX, distinct from the HALT-holders that veto.
3. **Teach the gates adaptivity** *(next)* — extend Studio Eyes to verify
   `prefers-contrast`/`prefers-color-scheme`/`forced-colors` and to grade *each shipped profile*,
   not one theme. Make *adaptivity* the next moat.
4. **Land one legible PoC win** *(next)* — make the Noticing collection the season and one build
   (Flok or CYL) the evidence — an adopter or an outcome — so month three has proof, not just engine.
