## What B is, and why it might be the stronger game

Design A **withholds** the leader's face to control face-bias *out* of a clean measurement of word-pull. Design B does the opposite on purpose: it **shows** the real historical leader's face so the halo fires, then confronts it with the record. You don't protect the player from the bias — you make them **feel it, then break it with evidence.**

Same spine, reframed: not *"measure the word-pull with the face controlled out,"* but *"make the player watch their own face-driven trust get re-priced by the facts."* A debiasing **intervention**, not a clean instrument.

This answers the founder's objection directly: a face doesn't break the game — it changes which game it is, and (per the evidence below) the confrontation version can move a person toward truth harder than withholding, **if the guardrails are right.**

## Scope (hard limits)

- **Three dead, historical, public-domain leaders only:** JFK (Oct 22, 1962), LBJ (Aug 4, 1964), Nixon (Nov 3, 1969). The face is the **real PD televised-address image** — the actual thing the era saw; attributed; not fabricated, not AI, not a deepfake.
- **Living-figure slots stay GATED and withheld.** Rights + disinformation are unchanged. B is **not** a license for living faces, ever.
- **"Never a real face" stays the default law** for Design A and every inclusive build. B is a **scoped, logged exception** for the three PD historical leaders — justified because here the face is the *treatment*, not a confound, and the subjects are dead, public-domain, and part of the historical record.
- **Bystanders/crowds:** the Osterweil guardrail holds — wide/anonymous/incidental PD only, never a close identifying portrait of a private person.

## Evidence base (from the literature; a methodologist must verify before any graded use)

- **Todorov** — face-based trait inference (~100ms) predicts elections. The halo B intends to elicit is real and fast.
- **Wood & Porter (2019); Nyhan (2021)** — the "backfire effect" is rare and non-robust; **most people update toward facts when corrected.** Supports confront-with-evidence.
- **van der Linden & Roozenbeek (inoculation / prebunking; the *Bad News* game)** — *experiencing* a manipulation inoculates better than being warned about it. Feeling the face-pull is the inoculation payload.
- **Lewandowsky & Ecker (Continued Influence Effect; *Debunking Handbook*)** — first impressions persist after correction; corrections work best when **active, repeated, and paired with an alternative causal account.** This dictates the guardrails.
- **Festinger (dissonance) + self-affirmation (Cohen; Nyhan)** — dissonance drives change **or** rationalization; **non-threatening framing decides which.**

*Net: the evidence leans toward B being effective — with guardrails — not toward "the face ruins it."*

## The beat loop (per era; **bold** = new vs. shipped Design A)

1. **Media prelude** — what the air already held. *(unchanged)*
2. **The room** — the period diorama, but the set now shows the **real PD broadcast image** (the actual televised face), not the withheld scan-line figure.
3. **Blind reaction** — rate trust on the words **+ the face**; the halo is *allowed to fire.* **This is the treatment, not a confound.**
4. **The record** — the evidence turns; what the room couldn't see.
5. **The naming (NEW)** — *"How much of that was the face, not the record?"* The mechanism made explicit. *(inoculation + CIE mitigation)*
6. **Re-react** — identical wording; rate again.
7. **The delta** — the two readings + the gap (the shipped mechanic-peak strip), now framed: **the gap is the halo, priced.**
8. **Coda** — the Saunders mercy line. *(anti-backfire self-affirmation)*

Repeat ×3 eras (spaced repetition). Final arc: three deltas + the pattern across the decade.
