# Down the Nerve v0.8.1: Coordinator seat (Horvath lens)

Played at 390x844, warm-up through end card, including the replay. Screenshots: `horvath/`. A seat modeled on published ideas, not the person.

## 1. What a learner believes after one ride

- **Correct:** a touch signal is fast (about 75 m/s). Damaged insulation slows it. A synapse is a gap between neurons.
- **Partly:** "Bright rings make it fast." Told; the spark glides, with no bare stretch to compare. "Stress means fewer spines." The player feels only a harder aim, and in-ride greens never change the spine count. "Spines grow back." Told, never shown.
- **Wrong:**
  - A test tomorrow slows my touch signal. The speedometer drops from 75 to 15 m/s before synapse 1, which breaks D12.
  - Stress is a thing to dodge, and hitting it is my fault.
  - A friend or a meal repairs nerve insulation.
  - A signal must aim at a spine, and a miss bounces it back.
  - Too much stress kills the signal (fade card).

## 2. The jump and landing

**What it should teach:** messengers cross the gap and the next neuron fires only when enough of them land. After a long hard stretch the thinking cortex has fewer spines, so it is harder to fire. The touch relays are unchanged, and the spines regrow.

**Why it fails now:**
- Five spines cover 67% of the tube, so the first three synapses pass mostly by luck and look the same.
- The stressed cortex (3 spines) covers 32%. That reads as "harder," not biology.
- The alarm pull is 16% of steering speed, too weak to feel.
- The spark teleports across the gap.
- A miss bounces the spark back, implying signals bounce off synapses.

**Redesign: "Fill the next neuron":**
- **The stop.** A no-pressure beat. The dendrite face fills the screen: lit spines, plus dashed stubs where spines are missing.
- **The release.** The spark bursts into about 10 messengers, and steering aims the spray. Messengers that hit lit spines fill a threshold ring; messengers at stubs pass through.
- **Relays 1-3.** Full spines; one release fires, with no hits or failure at any synapse. That contrast refutes "stress stops you feeling a touch."
- **The stressed thinking cortex.** One release half-fills the ring, which then fades. The player releases again, and again. The thorny alarm neuron beside it fires on its first spray. The feeling: same effort, less answer, while the alarm answers at once (Arnsten 2009; summation, OpenStax 12.5).
- **Regrowth.** Greens taken during leg 4 visibly sprout spines on the approaching face (Radley 2005, labeled as a simplification).

## 3. Connecting red and green to the ride

Today a cube sets band width, speed and (tray only) cortex spines; the HUD beam is frozen mid-ride.

Make it one variable:
- **Every cube lands on a live beam.** A spine preview beside the beam gains or loses spines as the beam tips, so the player carries a visible debt or credit into the cortex.
- **Reds arrive as the week deals them;** no dodging, no hits.
- **Greens are reached for.** Founder: this tension with "never homework" is yours to rule.
- **Each relay that fires slides the fulcrum** (skills, AFWI): the same cubes tip the beam less.
- **Only frayed bands count as hits.**

## 5. Claims to cut or reword

- **Week cards:** "Bad bands wider. Slower." and "- speed": cut.
- **Warm-up:** "Stress takes away places to land" plays with no landing, and "Supports clear the path" plays with no bands. Show these or cut them.
- **Replay, night card:** "short nights thin the landing places." The sources studied chronic stress, not sleep. Reword to: "A long run of hard weeks thins the landing places."
- **Friend card:** the spec marks it SOURCE OWED. Cut it until sourced.
- **Meal card:** "so the bands narrowed" has no mechanism; cut.
- **Practice** tells two stories (moves the fulcrum vs grows spines). Pick the fulcrum.
- **"Thinking fibers grow"** plays on the fingertip leg, within seconds. Show it only at the cortex, and add "over days to weeks."
- **Fade card:** reword to "Damaged insulation can stop a signal."
- **End card and Library:** "touch got through the first three relays the same." This is true only after #3.
- **Keep:** the alarm-protects line, spine regrowth in animals, 120 m/s.
