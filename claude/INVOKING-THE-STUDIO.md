# INVOKING THE STUDIO — what the founder says, and what must happen next

Ruled 2026-09-17, same session as THE CONVENING and LAWS-REGISTRY. This file is
the front-door script. It exists because "give this the full TSP treatment" was
a phrase every session interpreted for itself, and the interpretations drifted.

## THE THREE INVOCATIONS

**1. New build: "Convene <name>."**
Follow it with one sentence when you have it — what the player does, and what
you want them to notice. If you don't have the sentence yet, say so: drawing it
out of you IS step one, and nothing else happens until it exists. "Full TSP
treatment," "TSP treatment," and "convene" are the same invocation; any of them
triggers all of this.

**2. Revision: "Reconvene <name> on <what changed>."**
The seats answer the change, not the whole game again. If the change touches
the spine — what the game IS — the re-spine screen (OS §6 6.0.5) comes first:
new spine next to old, what it displaces, what survives.

**3. Repair: just say what's broken.**
A typo, a contrast miss, a dead link, a broken control — no convening. The belt
owns repairs. The boundary is one question: does the change alter what the
player does or notices? Yes → reconvene. No → fix it.

## WHAT THE SESSION OWES THE FOUNDER, IN ORDER

1. Echo the sentence back, or HALT asking for it. No sentence, no step two.
2. Seat the five standing seats — Coordinator, Stranger, Osterweil, Aleph,
   Studio Voice — plus the Conductor when anything will be seen, plus the
   build's own disciplinary bench (OS §6 0.5, Union Rep roll call included).
3. Write `convening/<name>-CONVENING.md`: the sentence, every seat's answer in
   its own voice, dissents preserved, SIGNED lines.
   (`convening-gate.py --template <name>` prints the blank form.)
4. **Show the findings to the founder BEFORE authoring anything.** He is the
   Calibrator; the tie-break is his. This is the whole point of the reorder:
   the seats inform the draft he receives, so iteration lands closer.
5. Author the draft against the findings file. Then the belt, then ship, each
   landing byte-verified same turn.

`convening-gate.py` enforces the file; the Aleph pass runs it at session start
and Integrity Guard step 6b runs it Mondays. A draft that arrives without its
findings file is out of process — the founder should say so in those words.

## WHY THE PHRASE ALONE FAILED

"Full TSP treatment" was bound to no hook — it meant whatever the session
already believed. The invocation now names a routine with a file at the end of
it, and two gates check the file. The founder's words did not change; what
listens to them did.
