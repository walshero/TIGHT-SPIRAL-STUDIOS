## WHAT SHIPPED AGAINST IT

`enjambment.html`, the Times Square skin, all CSS, no plates, no generated art. The
Legibility seat's HALT against Option B was real, so it was answered by execution and
then **measured rather than asserted**:

| Measured on the built page | Value |
|---|---|
| LED type against its housing | **13.0 : 1** (studio floor is 7:1) |
| Type luminance vs the brightest neon in frame | **7.0x brighter** |
| Brightest building | 0.003 luminance (effectively black) |

Four execution moves carry it, and every one exists to keep the poem the brightest
thing on the screen: the neon lives in the far plane, dim and glowing outward rather
than lit flat; the type sits inside a hard black bezel so nothing bright ever touches
the moving line; luminance is ranked rather than merely hue-separated, since real neon
at distance is chromatic but dark; and depth is built from static paint rather than
blur filters, which would have cost per frame behind a moving ticker.

One execution failure was caught and fixed in the same pass: the first skyline crossed
two repeating gradients to suggest windows and painted the whole lower half as a single
magenta mesh. A grid reads as a grid. Windows became columns of light in one direction,
spaced differently per tower, with roof lines catching the city glow so the stepped
silhouette reads.
