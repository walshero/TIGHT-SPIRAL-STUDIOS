# Rewilder SWOT — v1.15 (2026-11-26)

Founder ask: "SWOT on the current rewilder". Grounded in this session's builds (v1.2 to v1.15), the bot runs, preship, and the founder's rulings file.

## Strengths
- Whole game finishes end to end: both bot styles clear levels 1-3, the island, maze and pool party with zero script errors (v1.14 run). Preship SHIP, worst contrast pair 10.17, player-clear PASS.
- Three animals with distinct verbs (beaver climbs and smashes, turtle chomps and rips asphalt, salmon scouts, unlocks, swims the river). Tag any animal on screen.
- The thesis is mechanical, not a lecture: balanced PICK pairs at each site; the city's needs drive the pave crews.
- Salmon life cycle and the ocean scene: the founder's own 10/10 art bar.
- The finale reads as a story: red-and-black tower, electric maze, drones, valve, pool drain, Baron down the rainbow, pine bounce, hoe, pool-party hoops. Battle, victory and end music each have their own moment.

## Weaknesses
- Art is uneven, the founder's most repeated note. Open: level 2 city backdrop of repeating yellow X-braced bays (weak spot 3, not chosen yet); a ghost building overlay that appears over the dam and reservoir in some views.
- 300 ellPts/blobPts call sites. Most are real shapes; some are likely ovals standing in for things (new rule, 2026-11-26). Not swept.
- File is 745 KB, 4,885 lines, against the studio's 1 MB ceiling. Each art pass costs headroom.
- Newest systems have bot proof only, no founder play yet: the v1.12 puzzle-sheet maze, v1.13 river.
- Choreography breaks in ways the bots cannot see (Baron-and-rainbow logic took three builds). Bots check completion, not sense.

- Size ceiling: about 250 KB left.
- Regression risk in one 4,900-line file: cross-scene edits break things (the v1.4 orphan lines broke startGame). Bots catch crashes, not visual regressions.
- Cost: every build is screenshots + bots + preship + mirror.
