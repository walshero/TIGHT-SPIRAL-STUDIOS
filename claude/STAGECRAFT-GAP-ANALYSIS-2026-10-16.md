## B. Devices the research supports that v1 lacked
- **The trigger** (R5.3): an object or sound sets off visible feeling. Odysseus weeps at the lyre. A SHOW device for objects and moves.
- **The reaction** (R3, R7): the other character's response carries the information; Mamet's juxtaposition of uninflected images. Already used on Nicky's object card (Sol looks; Nicky doesn't), unnamed in v1.
- **Repetition and tempo** (R11): doing the same small thing again, or at the wrong speed.
- **The silent-movie test** (R2): a check for MOVE and OBJECT cards. If the card needs words to work, it is not a SHOW card.
- **Aristotle's ladder** (R5) as the spine of the whole game: TELL cards sit at rungs 1–2, SHOW cards at rungs 4–5.
