## INTERPRETED ASKS (the arcade lane's checklist — confirm any starred read)

1. **Game entries must read as GAMES, not icon-in-circle + text rows.** The
   cabinet art up top reads arcade-like; the game options themselves are still
   circle-icon-plus-label list furniture. Every playable should get real,
   thoughtfully produced game presentation — the same production standard the
   cabinets got. (*"the text and jam" is voice-to-text noise; the surrounding
   sentence is unambiguous: produced like games, not like a menu.*)
2. **Workshops are special and scheduled — move them OFF to the side.** They
   are appointments, not practice furniture; a player working through the
   practice parts should never trip over them. Visually and spatially distinct
   from the always-available games.
3. **The green growth on the cabinet tops must be legible.** A player should
   know what it is — name it, give it behavior, or tie it to the feed-the-beings
   system; unexplained decoration reads as noise. (Founder's own 08-09 ruling
   already gave this lane living beings; this asks that the flora join the same
   intentional world.)
4. **More ambient random events, more thoughtful animation.** He explicitly
   likes it when the random things happen — that delight is a feature to grow,
   not a garnish. More of them, better animated.
5. **More art direction overall** — "a little bit more art designed" applies to
   the whole surface, in the Samorost idiom already banked.
6. **Mushrooms: approved.** At least one mushroom in the flora is explicitly
   fine by the founder. Fits the Samorost pass natively.
