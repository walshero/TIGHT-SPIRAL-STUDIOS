## THE CRAFT VERDICT

**Playwriting.** The four rules the engine encodes are defensible and are the ones
that actually govern a two-hander: change of distance over absolute distance;
holding ground under an approach as the strongest single beat; the door as power;
stasis as death. The habituation rule, added after the meter pinned at 100 by beat
four, is the one that makes it teach rather than score, because it forces variety
and variety is what a scene actually needs.

**One real reservation, recorded rather than smoothed over.** The model has no
notion of *why* anyone crosses. In a real play the blocking is caused by the
objective; here the cross is the cause and the meaning is inferred. That is a
simplification the game does not currently admit to the player. It is defensible
for a practice cabinet and it would be wrong in a workshop.

**AI Skeptic, and this is the finding that blocks the stamp.** The eight lines are
machine-authored. The studio's own text policy says load-bearing prose in a game is
Matt's. The build declares this honestly in its footer and meta rather than hiding
it, which is the right call, but **a production build of a writing game cannot run
on scaffold prose written by the machine it is teaching students to outgrow.** The
lines are the one thing on the page that no gate can green.
