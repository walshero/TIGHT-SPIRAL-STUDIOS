# iSLO career/workforce lane — the three campuses, grounded

> **Source:** MassBay Fact Book (`massbay-fact-book-word.docx`, updated 2026-06-28, Drive `1011hDrfY63GRtRPXVYUW6h1L-tFEI9hb`, 5-year data through Fall 2024) + MassBay public pages (massbay.edu) + Data USA. Compiled 2026-08-08 for the career/workforce lane the studio staff flagged as the biggest strategic gap. **Last-verified:** 2026-08-08. Every number below is sourced; nothing invented (studio law).

## Why this lane (the gap, quantified)
The studio's iSLO suite so far speaks an English/writing idiom. But **Liberal Arts: English enrolled 26 students in Fall 2024** (Fact Book Table 1-16d), while the career/workforce divisions dwarf it. Fall 2024 enrollment by division (Fact Book Table 1-6):

| Division | Fall 2024 (AS + CER) | Trend 2020→2024 |
|---|---|---|
| **STEM** (CS, Engineering, Science & Math) | **1,558** (1,481 + 77) | 1,188 → 1,558 — up hard |
| **Business & Professional Studies** (Business, Early Childhood, Human Services, Legal/CJ) | **1,194** (992 + 202) | 907 → 1,194 — up |
| Humanities & Social Sciences | 561 | flat/again ~561 |
| **Health Sciences** | **381** (151 + 230) | 205 → 381 — up fast (Cert 145→230 in one year) |
| **Automotive Technology** | **143** (131 + 12) | 90 → 143 — up |
| Non-Degree / Undeclared | 741 | — |
| **Total** | **4,578** | 3,762 → 4,578 |

MassBay is **~69% part-time / commuter** (1,437 FT / 3,141 PT, Data USA 2024) — the single-file, offline, phone-first vehicle fits who these students are. **Every career division is growing; the humanities lane the studio started in is the smallest of them.** That is the case for this lane, in the college's own numbers.

## The three campuses (real, from the Fact Book masthead + campus pages)
- **Wellesley Hills — the flagship.** 50 Oakland Street. Chartered 1961; permanent campus since 1973. Home of the largest divisions (STEM, BPS, H&SS).
- **Ashland — the Automotive Technology Center.** 250 Eliot Street. The shop.
- **Framingham — the new campus, health-focused.** 490 Franklin Street. The Health Sciences, Early Childhood, and Human Services Center opened for **spring 2024**.

## Real partnerships & co-ops (sourced — the concrete hooks)
**Ashland / Automotive** (massbay.edu/academics/automotive, /automotive-all-brand, press 2025):
- **OEM-sponsored, product-specific programs: BMW, General Motors, JEEP/Chrysler/Dodge/Ram, Toyota/Lexus.** ASE Master-certified faculty.
- **Paid co-op** ("earn while they learn"); MassBay cites a **99% job-placement** rate for Automotive Academy graduates.
- The **All-Brand** program was selected for a **national pilot to advance technician training** (Oct 2025); a **Framingham High School automotive boot-camp** pipeline runs into it (2025).
- Plan mix (Fact Book 1-16b, Fall 2024): BMW 30, Toyota/Lexus 26, GM 21, Chrysler 20, All-Brand 34.

**Framingham / Health Sciences** (massbay.edu/center, /academics/health-sciences; framinghamsource.com):
- **Associate Degree Nursing + Practical Nursing**; the **Practical Nursing program was ranked #1 in Massachusetts (2023, PracticalNursing.org)**.
- **Clinical partners:** Beth Israel Deaconess, Boston Children's, Brigham & Women's, **MetroWest Medical Center**, and hospitals to Gardner/Worcester — real field placements and simulation labs.
- **Framingham State University 2+2 partnership** (transfer pathway), a MetroWest anchor.
- Health plan mix (Fact Book 1-16c, Fall 2024): Practical Nursing 30, Phlebotomy 21, Paramedicine 30, Medical Office Admin, Surgical Tech, etc.

**College-wide:** a **Corporate Partnerships, Workforce and Community Education** office (massbay.edu/cpwd) — the front door for employer partnerships and non-credit workforce training.

## Proposed first builds (iSLO-mapped, one per campus)
Each makes the iSLOs concrete *inside the program*, using a real on-the-job number/decision:
- **Ashland / Automotive — "Flat Rate" (iSLO 2 quantitative + iSLO 6 critical thinking + iSLO 3 tool use).** The flat-rate hour vs. real time; labor + parts + the diagnostic tree. The co-op "earn while you learn" number, made playable. Tightest identity, richest partnership story.
- **Framingham / Health — "The Med Math" (iSLO 2 quantitative + iSLO 6 + iSLO 1 SBAR handoff).** Dosage/rate calculation is the highest-stakes real number a nurse computes; pair it with an SBAR handoff (communication) and a dignity beat (iSLO 5). Newest campus, fastest growth, #1 program.
- **Wellesley / STEM — "Read the Data" (iSLO 2 + iSLO 3 + iSLO 6).** Biggest headcount (1,558) but broadest; a data-reasoning build reusing the Sticker Price / decomposition engine.

## Recommendation & open decision
All three are real and buildable. My lean for the **first** flagship: **Ashland / Automotive "Flat Rate"** — it has the most concrete partnership + co-op + placement story, the tightest campus identity, and it proves the studio's whole thesis ("your iSLOs live inside your program, not just in English"). **Framingham / Health "Med Math"** is an equally strong first if you want the newest, fastest-growing campus and the highest-stakes hook.

**For the founder:** which campus/program leads — **Ashland/Automotive**, **Framingham/Health**, or **Wellesley/STEM**? Then I scaffold that build to the studio floor (single-file, gated, dignity-first, sourced), grounded in these real partnerships.
