The Secret Humor Book (paw-written)

What is funny, to a cat. There are three things. Only three.

One. String that walks away on its own. Do not hurl it. Roll it, gently, straight along the ground.

Two. A fish, meeting a guest. Send the yarn up, steeply, and let the bowl introduce them.

Three. Everything, falling over at once. Steep and low. Commit completely.

Nothing else is funny. Do not ask about the water. We do not do water.
