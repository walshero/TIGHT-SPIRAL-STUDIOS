## THE DRIFT, NAMED

The 2026-08-05 reconciliation (TSP_Ledger) already ruled it: **The Viscosity is
CYL's felt-descent renderer, not a standalone peer game.** The player moves
through a Maslow-affected world — a world whose viscosity (the felt cost of
scarcity, the drag on every move) is created by the powers the player's leader
was enabled to use. Three months of builds and none of them show it: v5/v5-b
paint the descent as a readout, v6 as a static ladder, the full game as text
beats. The world never thickens under the player's feet. **That absence is the
drift.** The v7 build's first job is making the descent something the player
*moves through*, not something they are told about.

## THE ORDERS, UNPACKED INTO CHECKABLE REQUIREMENTS

1. **The retired tag is dead everywhere, in every variant.** Removed from all
   live surfaces 2026-08-08 (belt tick 6 guards it). THIS ruling closes the
   open question from that morning: the "corrected pitch" variant ("You don't
   judge the leader. You watch what in you decides to follow.", OS 2026-07-21,
   never built) falls under the same objection and is retired too. So does the
   period bible's paraphrase ("you judge what you were allowed to see"). The
   retired-lines blocklist now carries the second pattern. No thesis line
   exists for CYL today; if one is ever needed, the founder writes it.
2. **Founder's words only, wherever possible.** Game copy comes from his own
   words in docs and chats — never studio-drafted voice presented as his.
   Reachable sources (repo): `games-text-bank.md` (his authored texts, with its
   own extraction policy), his verbatim rulings quoted in `TSP_Ledger.md`,
   `choose-your-leader-map.md`, the bible docs' quoted-founder lines. Chats are
   machine-unreachable (the CYL-harvest access boundary) — anything needed from
   a chat, the founder pastes or it doesn't exist. Copy the studio drafted as
   placeholder must be TAGGED as placeholder-awaiting-founder-words, never
   passed off.
3. **Assets from the world, tagged.** The art lane already rules this:
   `cyl-period-bible.md` owns direction (photographic room + withheld figure,
   no real faces ever), `cyl-v5-image-lane.md` owns operations (magazine
   collage IS photoreal — real period print, cut and composited; AI as
   finishing assist only, always labeled; provenance carried per
   art-doctrine). v7 mounts these standing assets — it does not reinvent them.
4. **Screens are image-first.** The picture leads; words follow. This is the
   entry gate's own law (scene-first, >50% image floor) applied to EVERY
   screen, not just the entry.
5. **One thing at a time; build up when player skill is needed.** Each screen
   asks exactly one thing of the player. Complexity is earned scaffolding, not
   front-loaded controls. (The one-thing gate measures the entry; v7's design
   applies the same grammar to interior screens.)
6. **A full room is allowed.** Density in the SCENE is fine — TV broadcast,
   radio, the period furniture of a real room. The one-thing rule governs what
   the player is ASKED, not how alive the world is.
7. **Class representation and different perspectives, repped.** The same
   broadcast lands in different rooms: different class positions, different
   media apertures, different stakes. Grounding already in canon: the
   three-homes finding (`CYL_Harvest…md` §4 — a suburban TV household, a city
   apartment reading the Black press (Chicago Defender, Jet), a rural
   weekly-paper household, all hearing October 1962 differently). The Maslow
   descent (order #1) is NOT uniform across these rooms — the same enabled
   power thickens different worlds differently. That is the class-representation
   mechanic, not a checkbox.
8. **Roster: 3 + 3.** Cold War trio (JFK / LBJ / Nixon) — LIVE, records
   web-verified and dated. Current-era trio at today's truth — records REAL
   and sourced before they enter play, never fabricated (standing rule, OS
   §9). Roster truth per the OS's own record, not this doc's first guess:
   **Obama is already un-gated** (2026-06-27, the May 23 2013 NDU drone-war
   scene, sourced), so four scenes are LIVE today; **Trump and Biden remain
   gated with the even-handedness condition binding** — both need identically
   rigorous, identically critical sourced gaps before un-gating. Sourcing them
   is a research task for a network-capable session.
