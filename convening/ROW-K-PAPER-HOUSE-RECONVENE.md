## SEAT: Conductor

Two HALTs from this chair, both arithmetic.

**HALT 1 — the webfont.** The study loads Fraunces from Google Fonts. Fine on a
design canvas; a floor violation in the build. Canon's own header says it: single
file, offline, no network fetches, no external hosts. The Paper House display face
either ships EMBEDDED (subset woff2, base64, one weight) or it does not ship, and
Georgia bold carries the display. No third path. This is D2.

**HALT 2 — the study shows daylight only.** All six boards are the day stop. Row K
canon carries a six-stop forked ladder (three light, three dark under DARK HOST
LAW) and the backdrop-as-element fix, both bought with device-truth pain the same
week they were written. A Paper House rebuild that regresses either does not ship.
The dark side is not a filter over the day boards — a paper theater at night is its
own design (the house goes dark, the footlights do the work; glow becomes the main
light source, not an accent), and it wants its own boards before any build. The
runtime contrast audit — every screen, every stop, both emulations, computed
styles, plus the hostile-viewer repro — binds unchanged. Token gates passed Row K
three times while devices showed white-on-white; the gate is a suspect, the
founder's eyes are the oracle.

Watch-items, not HALTs: shadows replacing borders must keep every text-over-fill
pair at floor (the amber secret note and the green banner both compute clean in
the study — keep them literal); the footlight glow behind the ink plate must be
capped so plate strokes stay ≥4.5:1 over the brightest pixel of the glow at every
stop; text on rotated elements can soften on low-DPI screens — cap rotations at
±1° on anything a player reads, and never rotate body copy blocks more than that.

SIGNED 2026-09-21 — Conductor

## SEAT: Coordinator

The pass adds paint, and paint is load unless it encodes. Read each ornament by
the coherence principle: the ON STAGE stamp encodes selection — signal, keep. The
scene tints encode scene identity — ambience at best, since the player meets them
before knowing any scene; acceptable, but nothing may RIDE on them. The tape,
the perforation, the rotations encode nothing yet — they are texture, and texture
is fine exactly until it competes with the one decision on the screen. The study
keeps one-decision-per-screen intact; hold that in the build. Fraunces for
headings only, never body — a display face at body sizes taxes exactly the reader
this studio designs for. And the win condition of the whole redesign, stated so it
can fail: a student should notice the wedge and the empty cup MORE, not less,
because the set got beautiful. If playtest attention drifts to the curtains, the
set is upstaging the play and the pass has failed pedagogically while succeeding
aesthetically.

SIGNED 2026-09-21 — Coordinator

## SEAT: Stranger

I read the boards cold, as eyes that miss buttons. The real finding: the study
trades borders for shadows, and shadow is exactly what low vision loses first.
A button that is only a shadowed slab reads as decoration to me. The rule that
survives RP: every tappable thing keeps a computable edge — 2px minimum, ink on
paper — and the shadow is depth on top, never the affordance itself. The study's
HOME/BACK/COMFORT and the ticket cards currently fail this; the big ink buttons
pass (fill contrast is their edge). Second: the dashed left edge on choice cards
reads as "cut here," not "tap me" — and dashed borders already MEAN something else
in this game (Fran's box is dashed). One dialect per file. Third: the stamp works
— rotated, bordered, teal, high contrast, and it says ON STAGE in words, so the
selected state never rides on style alone. Keep the words forever. Fourth: 20px
body, 18px min, 48px targets all hold in the study — verified by reading, not
trusted. Nav floor holds. No opening wall — the lobby is a decision screen, not
a wall. Good.

SIGNED 2026-09-21 — Stranger

## SEAT: Osterweil

Nothing in the pass touches the mechanics, so my signature is about cost. The
restage loop is where the learning lives — RESTAGE, swap one card, CURTAIN again,
watch three rows re-react. Every second of ornament between the player and that
loop is a tax on the freedom to experiment. The curtain pull is already 2.2s and
it is charming ONCE; it must never play on a restage, only on first entry to a
scene (canon already scopes it to the reveal — keep that scoping through the
rebuild). Ink plates redraw on scene entry, not on every return. If the paper set
adds a single new wait to the swap-and-recurtain loop, this seat's signature
comes off.

SIGNED 2026-09-21 — Osterweil

## SEAT: Mamet

The set is now a character, which is correct — a paper theater says "someone made
this by hand for you," and that is the right promise for a game about staging.
But the rule I brought to the scenes binds the chrome too: objects earn their
place by being used. Audit of the study's hardware: the ON STAGE stamp is USED —
it does the selected-state's work. Earns its place. The tape on the secret note
is displayed, not used. Either the tape means something — the secret is a note
someone TAPED UP backstage, so let Fran's dashed box and the taped note share a
backstage-paper dialect — or cut it. The ticket perforation is displayed, not
used; a torn ticket that meant "spent" would earn it, but nothing is spent in
this game, so cut it (Stranger wants it cut anyway; two seats, one verdict). The
footlights are used — they light the plate. Keep. And one gift the pass should
take: the curtains gathering like pulled fabric is already the most Mamet thing
in the file — somebody's HANDS are in it. The paper set extends that. Do not
polish the hand out of it.

SIGNED 2026-09-21 — Mamet

## FINDINGS FOR THE CALIBRATOR — five decisions, none authored past

**D1 — Ship shape.** Recommended: **restyle in place** — `row-k-dress-rehearsal.html`
becomes v13 Paper House, same file, same mechanics, same telemetry-free spine.
Why: one canon file, no fork, the rename repair rides along. Tradeoff: the shipped
v12r2 look is gone from the live URL (git history keeps it). Simpler: ship nothing —
keep the study as reference for future builds only. Advanced: v13 plus extracting
the Paper House chrome as a reusable kernel for Reading Lamp and Sandbags.

**D2 — The display face (Conductor's HALT 1).** Recommended: **embed one subsetted
Fraunces weight** (700, latin subset, base64 woff2, ~40–70KB) with Georgia bold as
the declared fallback. Why: keeps the playbill voice inside the single-file law.
Tradeoff: file grows ~15%; the subset must be built once, on the Mac or in-session.
Simpler: Georgia bold everywhere — zero bytes, loses the playbill. Advanced: two
weights (600 + 700) for the scene-name hierarchy, ~2x the byte cost.

**D3 — The paper hardware (Mamet + Stranger, one verdict).** Recommended: **keep
the stamp, cut the perforation, tape only if it joins Fran's backstage dialect.**
Every tappable keeps a 2px ink edge; shadows are depth, never the affordance.
Why: objects earn their place by being used; affordance survives RP. Tradeoff:
slightly less "scrapbook" than the study. Simpler: stamp only, no tape anywhere.
Advanced: the backstage-paper dialect — secret note, Fran's box, and the verdict's
staged chips all read as one family of taped, clipped working paper.

**D4 — The three studio microcopy lines (Studio Voice).** Recommended: keep
"Doors open. Find your seat — Row K." (verbs present, retires the title into the
house); reverb "the prop table, before curtain" or cut it; founder rules whether
"a staging game" stands as a label or goes. Why: the verbless rule is his own.
Tradeoff: none — three lines. Simpler: cut all three, ship canon copy only.
Advanced: founder speaks three replacement lines and they land verbatim.

**D5 — The dark-side Paper House (Conductor's HALT 2).** Recommended: **design the
night boards before the build** — two boards minimum (Program + Secret/stage at the
warm-dark stop), house-goes-dark logic: deep brown papers, footlights as the main
light, CIE L* spacing on the three dark stops, backdrop as a real element. Why:
Row K's own history — token gates passed while devices showed white-on-white; the
dark side is a design, not a filter. Tradeoff: one more canvas pass before any
build. Simpler: build daylight first, HALT at the gate until dark boards exist
(the gate will hold it anyway). Advanced: all five screens boarded at warm-dark
plus black, and the comfort-kernel patch extracted from the result.
