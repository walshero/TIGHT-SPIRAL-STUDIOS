## SEAT: Coordinator

*Horvath, on the lever 1 question.*

**The question put:** should lever 1 become four reward cards the operator chooses
between (a stranger's like, Mom's comment, Quinn's view, Rosa's like), or stay one Boost
with varied rewards behind it?

**Ruling: one Boost. The variety goes behind the button, not in front of it.**

1. **The cards change the construct.** Lever 1 teaches a variable-ratio schedule: the
   payout is unpredictable and the checking is not. The reveal already measures exactly
   that ("you tapped Boost N, it paid off M, about one in K"). Four cards turn a schedule
   into a choice. A player choosing rewards is sorting, and a sort has no ratio. The
   telemetry would stop measuring the thing the lever names. That is the blurred-construct
   failure the 2026-09-13 screen-grammar ruling fixed once already.
2. **Lever 1 is the onboarding lever.** It carries the hire-screen frame (role, one metric,
   the mechanic named honestly). Four options on the first interaction is extraneous load
   at the moment germane load matters most. The founder's own complaint ("too many things
   live at once", July) is this.
3. **The founder's real ask survives, and gets stronger.** "Different types of rewards"
   is honored by making the payout vary in KIND and SIZE as well as in timing: nothing,
   a stranger's like, Mom's comment, Hank's like, Quinn viewing her story. Each payout is
   named on Tess's card as it lands, so the player perceives the lanes. Tess comes back
   faster after some than others, and the card says how fast.
4. **The discovery becomes a measurement, not a guess.** The reveal states which currency
   pulled her back fastest (Quinn's view) and which one never arrived on the schedule
   (Rosa's like). That is the machine's construct, so it is teal; the player's own taps
   stay plain ink.
5. **Rosa's like is not a card.** It is withheld from lever 1 entirely and arrives at
   12:06, buried. A reward the player could hand out would spend the turn.

**Numbers (so it is checkable):** six taps from 26% to the 72% line. Schedule by tap:
none (+5), stranger like (+8), Mom's comment (+8), none (+5), Quinn's view (+16), none
(+5). Three payouts, three lanes, the largest jump and the fastest return on Quinn.
SIGNED Coordinator (Horvath), 2026-10-13

## SEAT: Osterweil

Freedom to fail: no lever can be lost, only slowed; an unpaid Boost costs nothing but a
tap, which is the point of a variable schedule. Freedom to experiment: the player can tap
past the line and watch the schedule keep paying unevenly. Freedom of identity: the
operator is a role the player steps into knowingly, never a verdict on them. The 12:06
beat is not a punishment of the player; it is something they notice.
SIGNED Osterweil, 2026-10-13
