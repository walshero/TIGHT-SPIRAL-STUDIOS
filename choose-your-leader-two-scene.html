Choose Your Leader · two rooms, two nights

You are in the chair.

Two rooms, fifty-nine years apart. In each one the news is on, the paper is out, and a decision is about to leave the room. You will carry three things in, issue one order, and then hear what the room could not hear when it decided.

About six minutes a room. Nothing you do here is scored, sent, or stored. Sound is off unless you turn it on, and everything spoken is also written.

Kennedy, October 22, 1962 · then Georgia, January 2, 2021

For instructors

Choose Your Leader is a situational judgment exercise. It records what you decided, what you could see when you decided it, and whether you changed your mind when the record opened. It reports those as a description of your reasoning, not as a score, and it does not claim to measure or predict leadership ability.

Every claim in the fog is real, dated, and attributed. The two second frames are records, not opinions: for 1962, the declassified file; for 2021, the courts, the Justice Department, the state's own audits, and a recording. Nothing about the player is stored or transmitted.
