# Kernels — from the fireground Aleph panel (2026-08-03)
*Studio memory (Funes). Small, sourced, reusable claims distilled from a three-seat panel (image provenance · learning sciences · fire-domain + dignity), convened for the "Reading the Fireground" reskin. Per OS §6.4: claim · provenance · confidence · conditions · stamp. These are cross-build, not fireground-only.*

---

### K1 — Realism-carrying documentary builds are the licensed-photo lane, not cut-paper
**Claim:** When a build's meaning depends on *genuine real-world realism* (a documentary of real events, real people, real record), the Medium Gate sends it to the **licensed-photo lane** — studio-drawn art is a gate violation, and for events where people died it is also a dignity breach.
**Provenance:** OS §3.2 Medium Gate (Q1); founder ruling 2026-08-03 ("no studio created art when lives matter"). **Confidence:** verified (studio doctrine + founder). **Conditions:** applies when realism is load-bearing; does NOT apply to games where people-as-roles / inclusivity is the point (that's cut-paper's job). **Stamp:** 2026-08-03.

### K2 — Federal fire imagery is legally clean *only for staff-authored figures*; self-host
**Claim:** USFA Fire Service Image Gallery (copyright-free federal) and DVIDS/NIST/NIOSH **staff-authored** photos are public/usable; but NIST/NIOSH reports routinely embed third-party figures captioned "courtesy of [a fire dept / individual / media]" that are **NOT** cleared by the federal-work rule — check each caption. **Self-host** (download+commit); do not hotlink (USFA 403s bots). **UL FSRI is permission-gated (excluded).** Municipal "San Diego Reading Smoke" drill imagery is **Dave Dodson's copyrighted** *Art of Reading Smoke* — a study reference, not an image source.
**Provenance:** 17 USC §105; usfa.fema.gov/gallery; dvidshub.net copyright policy; nist.gov/oism/copyrights; fsri.org/terms; panel provenance seat. **Confidence:** verified (with the hard per-figure caption caveat). **Conditions:** any build sourcing documentary photography. **Stamp:** 2026-08-03.

### K3 — Perceptual size-up training transfers via predict-then-reveal on a real schema
**Claim:** "Noticing"/perceptual-expertise transfers (not recalls) when learners classify many short varied cases with **prediction-then-reveal**, **contrasting cases** (look-alikes that read differently), **faded scaffolding**, and **calibration** feedback — and when the image's judgment lives *in the frame*. Use the fire service's own read schema, **B-SAHF** (Building·Smoke·Air track·Heat·Flame; Hartin) + **Dodson's Reading Smoke** (volume·velocity·density·color), for BOTH the learner's read and the record reveal (like-for-like).
**Provenance:** Kellman PALMs (kellmanlab.psych.ucla.edu/files/kellman_2013.pdf); Dodson (fireengineering.com); Hartin B-SAHF (cfbt-us.com/wordpress/?p=878); calibration (lifescied.org/doi/10.1187/cbe.18-10-0202). **Confidence:** verified (peer-reviewed + fire-service canon). **Conditions:** any perceptual/judgment-under-uncertainty trainer. **Stamp:** 2026-08-03.

### K4 — LODD imagery dignity floor
**Claim:** For training on incidents where people died: use investigation/structure/smoke/construction/aftermath-**without-victims** imagery only; never victims, bodies, identifiable casualties, or flames-as-spectacle ("fire porn"). Frame as memorial + instruction ("the lessons were paid for in these lives"), never "part of the job." Caption every image with source; never launder provenance or present a render as a scene photo.
**Provenance:** NFFF (firehero.org/resources/handling-a-lodd); panel fire-domain/dignity seat; extends founder-canon §3.1 dignity-first floor. **Confidence:** verified. **Conditions:** any build using imagery of real harm/death. **Stamp:** 2026-08-03.
