## THE GAG
The cold-open physics scene is now the myth. A mountain; **Sisyphus** waits wearily at its base with his boulder; a **taskmaster** stands partway up the slope, arm out, **pointing at him** — *roll it up.* You **tap to drop the boulder**; it rolls down the mountain and **flattens the taskmaster** to a pancake (X eyes, limbs splayed) — and **Sisyphus throws his arms up, laughing.** Tap again: the boss stands back up, and gets flattened again. The eternal loop, turned into the joke.

## WHY IT'S RIGHT (not just a gag)
- **Camus.** *"One must imagine Sisyphus happy."* The whole myth is the absurd condition — meaningless labor commanded by an unseen authority. The game's move is to make him *laugh*, and to laugh by flattening the one doing the pointing. That is the absurd hero's freedom, staged as slapstick.
- **It IS the humor model.** Flattening the pointing authority is **Superiority** (we laugh at the overseer's downfall) held **benign** (cartoon pancake, deserved, no real harm) — and it's **pure physical comedy**, the *universal* register the model names as the floor that lands in any room, no shared language required. The cold open literally demonstrates the thesis the game then teaches.
- **Tone-setting.** It tells the player in five wordless seconds what this studio's comedy is *about*: comedy as a small rebellion against being told what to do. Keaton (Buster, room #1) and Camus (the cold open) rhyme — physical laughter as dignity under absurd conditions.

## RELATIONSHIP TO BUSTER
Two different roles, no conflict: **Sisyphus** is the *cold-open sight-gag* (the thesis vignette). **Buster** (the sleepy cat) remains *room #1* — the honest audience you read in the game proper. The cold open sets the tone; the rooms are the game.

## HOW IT'S BUILT (single-file, offline, accessible)
- Real Matter.js: the mountain slope is a physics ramp; the boulder is a rigid body; the taskmaster is a sensor the boulder passes through, tripping the flatten. Deterministic enough to read every time.
- Drawn in our line-art on the theme-aware canvas (mountain hatched, stick-figures, a rock with facets).
- Reduced-motion / Clear Reader: a **static tableau** (boulder at the peak, taskmaster pointing, Sisyphus weary) — no motion, meaning intact. The old SVG machine was removed; the canvas is the single source.
